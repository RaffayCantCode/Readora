# Readora Product Context

Readora is a personal digital library for people who want their digital books to feel owned, curated, and alive. It is not a bookstore and it is not a generic PDF reader. The product helps readers discover books, organize their own collection, and enter reading experiences that feel more physical and memorable than opening a document.

Primary users are serious casual readers, collectors, students, and people with large digital libraries. They need speed when searching, but the signature experience should feel like browsing a beautifully designed archive.

Default register: product surface with brand-level craft. The first screen must be usable as an app, while still feeling memorable enough to define the brand.

Personality: editorial, tactile, intelligent, calm, slightly cinematic.

References:
- Film archive interfaces with centered hero objects and lateral browsing.
- Type specimen shelves with strong book spines and confident color.
- Stripe Press physical book stacks: precise, restrained, object-focused.
- Quiet reading apps with clean utility controls and generous spacing.

Anti-references:
- Generic SaaS dashboards.
- Fake 3D rooms made from flat brown rectangles.
- Beige-only palettes.
- Decorative motion that does not reveal state or object behavior.
- Repetitive cards without hierarchy.

Accessibility needs:
- Reduced motion must remain available.
- Classic mode must remain fast and readable.
- Interactive books must be keyboard-focusable and screen-reader named.
